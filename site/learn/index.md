# Learn

Every project on this site leans on a dozen tools. Here's how to learn any of them at the depth you need, and what I
studied myself.

## Crash course

A course on every technology used here, at three levels:

| Level | Time | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Familiar** | 15 minutes | Enough to follow a conversation: what it is, where it fits, the trade-offs |
| **Beginner** | 1 hour | A small project you build and run yourself |
| **Intermediate** | 4 hours | Deeper material and a scored test |

Sign up with your email, track your progress, and unlock the next level when you pass the quiz and finish the
project. Quizzes have unlimited retries. A model drafts the courses and runs the first review; I approve every level
before it goes live.
