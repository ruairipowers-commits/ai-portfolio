# RB-12 NAV sign-off delay and escalation

## Symptoms
NAV sign-off target (20:00 ET) is at risk because of late or missing feeds or open breaks.

## Likely causes
- Late critical feeds; unresolved position or P&L breaks.

## Steps
1. List open critical breaks for the date and their owners.
2. Give the NAV team an expected sign-off time every 30 minutes.
3. If sign-off will miss 22:00 ET, the ops lead decides whether to publish an estimated NAV.

## Escalation
The ops lead owns the publish decision; the on-call engineer never publishes.
