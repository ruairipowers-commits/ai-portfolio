# RB-13 Restating a published NAV

## Symptoms
A break is found after NAV was published for the date.

## Likely causes
- A late correction to prices, FX or positions.

## Steps
1. Quantify the NAV impact per share class.
2. The ops lead and the CFO's office decide on restatement under the fund's error policy.
3. Never rerun or republish a published NAV without that approval.

## Escalation
Always the ops lead and the CFO's office.
