To be honest, we built this because our own reconciliations were taking two days a month. At the end of the day, the problem is simple: the data arrives late and nobody owns the breaks. Basically, we route every break to an owner within an hour. We ran it for six months with three pilot teams, and to be honest, the results surprised us: breaks closed in four hours instead of two days. Going forward, we want ten teams on it by June. At the end of the day, it's about ownership.
