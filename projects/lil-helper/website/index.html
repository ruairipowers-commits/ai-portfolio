Help & Answers

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the Pro Portal, finding clients, and client management.

What is the Dinner Peace Pro Portal? +
The Dinner Peace Pro Portal is a mobile-first operating system designed specifically for personal chefs managing multiple households. It unifies client profile dossiers, batched multi-store shopping runs, bulk protein splitting, credit card rewards reimbursement tracking, 4x6 thermal sticky label printing, and hands-free voice field entry.
How do chefs and families find each other? +
Families can say they’re looking for a chef and what they’d pay; Pro chefs list a profile with their rates, what they cook and how far they travel. Each side searches by distance and sends a request to meet. Until a request is accepted, chefs see only the family’s town, how many people and children, and the allergens to cook around — never names or a street address. Switching chefs is fine at any time.
How does 4x6 thermal sticky label printing work? +
Dinner Peace Pro connects directly to wireless AirPrint thermal label printers (such as Rollo, Brother, or Phomemo) to print high-contrast, waterproof 4x6 adhesive labels for glass container lids. Each label details the dish name, container number, use-by date, allergen status, and exact oven/stovetop reheat temperatures.
How does card rewards routing and client reimbursement work? +
If you prefer to earn points or cashback on your personal/business cards (like Amex Gold for 4x groceries or Chase Sapphire), Dinner Peace Pro automatically itemizes receipts and generates automated reimbursement invoices per household. Or a client can pay with their own card: their items go through a separate checkout. Dinner Peace never stores card numbers.
What is the Coverage Pack for substitute chefs? +
When you travel or take time off, you can generate a password-protected "Coverage Pack" link that opens only between the dates you choose (1 to 31 days) for a trusted colleague. It provides temporary access to client gate codes, alarm instructions, emergency numbers, allergen matrices, and daily recipe workflows without exposing sensitive financial records.
